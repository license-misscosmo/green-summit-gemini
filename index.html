01THE PURPOSE

Rooted in who you are.
Moving toward what’s next.

Cosmo Green Summit 2026 by Nam A Bank brings the diverse cultural identities of Miss Cosmo contestants together. Each contestant represents a country, a story, a set of values, and a community-impact project.

Identity is not static. It evolves through culture, technology, and people — from local values to global connections. Nam A Bank serves as a bridge between identity, technology, finance, and communities, bringing stories and initiatives onto an international stage.

From identity, we connect.
From connection, we create impact.
